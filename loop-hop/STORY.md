# Loop Hop — Story and Plot

## Premise

Pip is a bright yellow runner with a wonderfully bad habit: every time they finish a lap, they decide they can manage one more.

High above the city hangs the Loop, a glowing circular track where runners chase personal records. There is no finish line. Your run lasts as long as you can keep going. Pip has arrived for a quiet practice session and found one problem: a red spike sticking out of the track.

Going around means meeting it again. And again. And a little faster each time.

## Plot

Pip steps onto the Loop and starts running. The first circuit looks easy. The spike comes into view, Pip jumps, and a clean landing carries them into their second lap.

Now the track feels quicker. The same obstacle approaches from a different angle, and the easy rhythm becomes something Pip has to earn. Each successful circuit adds another point to the record. The challenge grows without introducing a new enemy or changing the goal: keep moving, clear the spike, finish another lap.

Eventually, Pip clips the spike. There is a brief burst of yellow sparks, the run stops, and the final score appears beside the personal best.

Pip reforms at the starting point. The spike is still there. The record is still waiting.

One tap starts the next attempt.

## How the story appears in play

- The opening screen shows Pip beside the Loop with the instruction **“Tap to jump.”**
- Pip's movement and small landing reactions provide personality without dialogue.
- Each completed lap produces a short pulse around the ring and adds one point.
- A collision is a harmless arcade reset, expressed through sparks and a quick reappearance.
- The ending of every run is the same story beat: Pip takes another shot at the record.

## Tone and boundaries

Confident, energetic, and lightly funny. Pip's stubbornness is the character's defining trait.

The story lives in the premise and the animation. It does not require cutscenes, conversations, a campaign, or an explanation before the first jump. The player's improving score supplies the ongoing arc.
