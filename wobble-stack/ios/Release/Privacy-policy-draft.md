# Wobble Stack privacy

Wobble Stack runs entirely on your device. The game does not collect or transmit personal information and does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, purchases, or third-party SDKs.

The game saves your best score locally on your device so it can display the score when you return. Scores are not sent to a server or synced between devices. Deleting the app removes its local app data.

When you test the app using Apple's TestFlight, Apple handles beta feedback and diagnostic information under its own privacy terms. Those services are separate from the offline game.

Before using this text for a public release, add the developer's public support contact and publish it at a stable privacy-policy URL. This file is a release draft; it has not been published or entered into App Store Connect.
