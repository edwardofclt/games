Wobble Stack — beta 1.0 (2)

UPDATE: Corrected the app icon. Gameplay is unchanged.

WHAT TO TEST
Tap to drop the sliding jelly block. Each settled block adds a point. Aim carefully:
overhanging blocks and weight above a lower joint can topple the tower.

Please try:
• Touch timing, especially on smaller iPhones and larger iPads.
• Dropping blocks near both edges and balancing a crooked tower.
• Pause / resume, leaving and returning to the app, and rotating the device.
• Best-score persistence after closing and reopening the app.
• Retry after a miss or collapse.

The game runs offline with no login, advertising, purchases, or analytics.

BETA REVIEW NOTES
This is a complete single-mechanic arcade game. All gameplay assets and code are
bundled in the app. It requires no account, demo credentials, backend, or internet
connection. No in-app purchases or external links appear in the game. Tap anywhere
on the playfield to drop a block. Tap II to pause, and Keep stacking to resume.
A failed run offers Stack again. The best score is stored locally on the device.

KNOWN BETA LIMITS
Silent gameplay. Scores do not sync between devices. A run is paused when the app
is inactive but is not restored after the operating system terminates the process.
The support model is an intentional center-of-mass approximation, not full physics.
Physical device playtesting is still needed; touch and lifecycle tests have passed
in both iPhone and iPad simulators.
