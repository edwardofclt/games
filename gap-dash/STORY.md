# Gap Dash — Story and Plot

## Premise

Zip is a blue courier spark working the shortest delivery route in the world: from one wall to the other.

Between those walls sits an old signal gate. Its opening slides up and down continuously. Zip cannot steer during a crossing, so every delivery comes down to a single decision: when to go.

Other couriers take their time. Zip wants the station's crossing record.

## Plot

Zip waits on the left landing pad, watching the opening move. For a moment, the route looks clear. Zip launches, streaks through the gate, and snaps onto the right pad. One successful crossing. One point.

The next delivery is already waiting. Zip turns to face the other wall. The route is familiar, but the gate is moving faster, and the timing that worked a moment ago will not necessarily work again.

The run becomes a series of small commitments. Wait a little longer, or trust the opening that is about to arrive? A near miss makes the next crossing feel possible. A clean streak makes the record feel close.

Eventually, Zip launches at the wrong moment and bumps the gate. The spark scatters, the station catches it, and the attempt ends. The final crossing count appears beside the best score.

Zip reforms on the starting pad, already watching the opening.

## How the story appears in play

- The first screen places Zip on a wall opposite the landing pad, with the instruction **“Tap to cross.”**
- Zip briefly stretches in the direction of travel before snapping across the screen.
- A successful landing gives a crisp flash, sound, and score increase.
- After each crossing, Zip automatically faces back toward the opposite wall.
- Colliding with the gate ends the run with a short, harmless spark effect and an immediate route back to play.

## Tone and boundaries

Quick, precise, and a little cocky. Zip looks eager to launch even when waiting would be wiser.

The delivery job explains the repeated crossings without adding packages to manage, destinations to select, or a separate mission system. The only ongoing plot is Zip's attempt to turn a simple route into an extraordinary streak.
